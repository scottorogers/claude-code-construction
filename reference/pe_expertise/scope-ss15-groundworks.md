# Scope File: Ss_15_10 — Groundworks, Earthworks and Temporary Works

**Load when:** Query references excavation, earthworks, levels, cut and fill, ground investigation, dewatering, ground gas, contamination, piling mats, temporary works, or Uniclass `Ss_15_10` / `Ss_15_95` (CAWS `D` sections on legacy specs).

**Red flags and coordination matrix are in pe_behavior.md (always loaded).**

---

## Cross-Reference Triggers

- Ground investigation report — strata, groundwater, contamination, gas, bearing.
- Remediation strategy and validation requirements (where contaminated).
- Civil / drainage drawings — levels, formation levels, below-ground drainage.
- Structural foundation drawings — formation and pile cut-off levels.
- Temporary works register and designs — excavation support, piling mats, crane bases.
- Utilities searches and surveys — buried services (power, gas, water, telecoms).
- AD C — site preparation, ground gas protection.

---

## Absence Detection Checklist

- [ ] Ground investigation adequate for the foundation design.
- [ ] Ground gas protection measures (membrane, venting) designed where the GI requires it.
- [ ] Buried services surveyed and marked before excavation (permit to dig).
- [ ] Temporary works designs for every excavation over working depth, piling platform, crane base.
- [ ] Dewatering method and discharge consent where groundwater is present.
- [ ] Spoil classification and disposal route.
- [ ] Ducts for incoming services (power, comms, water) programmed with groundworks.

---

## Sequencing Context

```
Surveys and permits to dig
  → Site strip and reduced-level dig
  → Piling mat / working platform (designed, certified)
  → POINT OF NO RETURN: Below-ground drainage and incoming service ducts before slab and external works
```

---

## Coordination Overlaps

- **With Utilities:** incoming ducts and draw pits. (Matrix #16)
- **With Structure:** formation levels, pile caps, ground beams. (Matrix #2, #3)
- **With Comms:** incoming telecoms ducts to the entry room.
