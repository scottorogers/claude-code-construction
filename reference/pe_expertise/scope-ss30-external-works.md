# Scope File: Ss_30_14 / Ss_45 — External Works and Landscaping

**Load when:** Query references external paving, kerbs, car parks, roads, fencing and gates, soft landscaping, street furniture, external lighting, or Uniclass `Ss_30_14` Paving / `Ss_30_45` Pavement covering / `Ss_25_14` Fence systems / `Ss_45` Flora and fauna (CAWS `Q` sections on legacy specs).

**Red flags and coordination matrix are in pe_behavior.md (always loaded).**

---

## Cross-Reference Triggers

- External works layout and levels; drainage layout.
- Accessible routes from site boundary and parking to entrances (AD M Vol 2 ramps — `reference/ad_m_vol2_access.yaml`).
- External lighting and CCTV columns, EV charging bays (AD S).
- Incoming services routes and draw pits.
- Planning conditions (landscaping, tree protection, lighting).

---

## Absence Detection Checklist

- [ ] Accessible route gradients and landings checked against AD M Vol 2 Table 1 and para 1.26.
- [ ] Ducts for external lighting, CCTV, access control gates, EV chargers laid before surfacing.
- [ ] Draw pits and chambers for comms and power at changes of direction.
- [ ] Fire service vehicle access route and hydrants (AD B).
- [ ] Planning condition discharge for landscaping and lighting.

---

## Sequencing Context

```
Below-ground drainage and ducts → sub-base → kerbs → surfacing → soft landscaping
  → POINT OF NO RETURN: Surfacing laid over missing ducts
```
